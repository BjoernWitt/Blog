FAQ

What’s the story behind my travel blog?

It all started with a feeling of restlessness and a one-way ticket. I didn’t launch this blog to showcase a flawless, curated life of luxury. I started it because I wanted to capture the raw, unfiltered reality of exploring the world—the missed trains, the breathtaking sunrises, the street food that changed my life, and the incredible people who turned strangers into family.
